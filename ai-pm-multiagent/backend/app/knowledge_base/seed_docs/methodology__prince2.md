# Methodology Guide: PRINCE2

Best suited for projects requiring strong governance, a formal Project Board,
staged delivery with defined management stages, and clear business
justification tracked throughout (the Business Case). Common in public sector
and large enterprise programs.

Signals: "governance", "stage gate", "board", "PRINCE2", "formal oversight",
"public sector", "business case".

Recommended artifacts: Project Initiation Document (PID), Stage Plans, Highlight
Reports, End Stage Reports, Business Case.
