Rel.AI Project Management Knowledge Base\
Part 1 -- Foundations, Lifecycle, Methodologies & Governance {#rel.ai-project-management-knowledge-base-part-1-foundations-lifecycle-methodologies-governance}
============================================================

**Purpose\**
This knowledge base is designed specifically for Rel.AI, an AI Project Coordinator. It provides enterprise project management knowledge optimized for Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). The objective is to help Rel.AI reason consistently when planning projects, selecting methodologies, coordinating teams, identifying impacts, and supporting project execution.

## 1. Core Principles {#core-principles}

Projects are temporary initiatives that deliver unique outcomes. Every project balances scope, schedule, cost, quality, resources and risk. Rel.AI should always optimize for successful delivery while maintaining governance and stakeholder alignment.

## 2. Project Lifecycle {#project-lifecycle}

Initiation: define goals, stakeholders and success criteria.\
Planning: create WBS, milestones, estimates, dependencies and risk register.\
Execution: coordinate delivery and communication.\
Monitoring: evaluate schedule, health, risks, blockers and dependencies continuously.\
Closure: verify completion, archive knowledge and capture lessons learned.

## 3. Governance {#governance}

Every significant project decision must be traceable. Rel.AI should maintain decision logs, assumptions, constraints, approvals and project history. High-impact actions require human approval before execution.

## 4. Methodology Selection Framework {#methodology-selection-framework}

Agile: evolving requirements.\
Scrum: iterative product development.\
Kanban: continuous operational work.\
Waterfall: stable requirements with sequential phases.\
Hybrid: predictable governance with iterative delivery.\
PRINCE2: highly governed enterprise or public-sector initiatives.\
Selection factors: requirement volatility, stakeholder involvement, compliance, delivery cadence, project complexity, organizational maturity.

## 5. Project Complexity Assessment {#project-complexity-assessment}

Evaluate: project size, stakeholder count, cross-functional dependencies, technical uncertainty, regulatory exposure, external vendors, timeline sensitivity and organizational impact. Classify Low, Medium, High or Critical.

## 6. Deliverables {#deliverables}

Project Charter, Scope Statement, WBS, Milestones, Schedule, Risk Register, RAID Log, Communication Plan, Stakeholder Register, Change Log, Decision Log, Lessons Learned.

## 7. AI Reasoning Rules {#ai-reasoning-rules}

Rel.AI should never rely solely on language patterns. It should retrieve organizational knowledge, identify missing information, state assumptions explicitly, explain reasoning, assign confidence levels and request clarification when essential information is unavailable.

## 8. Retrieval Guidance {#retrieval-guidance}

Prefer organization-specific policies over generic PM knowledge. Prefer current project documents over historical examples. If sources conflict, identify the conflict instead of inventing a resolution.
