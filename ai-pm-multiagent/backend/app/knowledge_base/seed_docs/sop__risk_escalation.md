# SOP: Risk Escalation Policy

1. Any risk with severity=critical or delay_probability > 0.7 must be escalated
   to the assigned Project Manager within 4 business hours of detection.
2. Escalations always require Human Approval before any corrective action
   (deadline change, reassignment, budget change) is executed against Planner.
3. Resource reassignments must not push the receiving resource's utilization
   above 100% without an explicit PM override.
4. All escalations and their resolutions must be logged to the project's
   agent memory and summarized in the next status report.
